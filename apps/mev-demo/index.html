MEV Creator

Standalone sandwich-attack simulator for Sepolia. Connect ANY wallet — it becomes the victim of a real front-run / swap / back-run trio against the MEVTEST pool. No private keys are ever typed; the connected wallet signs its own swap.

This tool only CREATES attacks. It is not part of any insurance product — what you do with the resulting transaction hashes is up to you.
