How it works

  

    What this is: a standalone tool that creates a REAL sandwich attack
    (front-run → your swap → back-run) on the Sepolia testnet. Your connected
    wallet is the victim — just like a trader getting MEV'd on a DEX. It exists
    to produce genuine attack transactions on demand for demo purposes.


    Steps:

    1. Click "Connect browser wallet" and pick your Sepolia account.

    2. Click "Sign swap & run attack" and confirm the swap in your wallet.

    3. Wait ~30–60 seconds while the attack wraps your swap, then copy the
    victim tx hash shown here.


    Inspect any transaction:

    "Detect" answers "was this transaction part of a sandwich attack?" —
    it prints a report showing the front-run and back-run hashes around the
    victim swap, the attacker's address, and which rules passed
    (classification: SANDWICH means a verified attack).

    "Simulate loss" answers "how much did the attack cost the victim?" —
    it prints what the victim actually received vs. what they would have
    received without the front-run, and the difference is the verified loss
    (in raw token units).

    Both outputs are raw JSON. A successful look always ends with
    "classification": "SANDWICH" and a non-zero "loss".
