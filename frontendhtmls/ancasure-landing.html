Verification

We don't just trust a database

AncaSure verifies on-chain evidence cryptographically through Creditcoin's Attestcoin infrastructure before it's ever used to decide a claim.

That means the attacker transaction, your transaction, the back-run, their ordering in the block, and the pool's swap activity are all confirmed before a payout is calculated, not assumed from an explorer query.
